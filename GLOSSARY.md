# Chazin Lab website

The public website of the Chazin Lab, written mainly for prospective graduate students and postdocs.

## Research

**Theme**:
One of the lab's broad research areas, such as Genome Maintenance or Innate Immunity. Every project belongs to exactly one theme.
_Avoid_: Area, topic, focus, program

**Project**:
A specific line of research within a theme, shown with a representative figure and a short description.
_Avoid_: Study, research item

## People

**Member**:
Someone currently working in the lab, including the PI.
_Avoid_: Staff, team member, current person

**Role**:
A member's position in the lab, such as Postdoctoral Fellow. The roles form one ranked list, which sets the order of members on the People page.
_Avoid_: Title, position, rank

**Alumnus**:
A former member, listed with their years in the lab and where they are now. The plural is alumni.
_Avoid_: Former member, past member, graduate

**PI**:
The principal investigator who leads the lab: Walter Chazin.
_Avoid_: Head, director, lab leader

## Publications

**Publication**:
A paper indexed in PubMed with the PI as an author, excluding errata and preprints.
_Avoid_: Article, citation

**Key paper**:
A publication chosen to represent a particular project.
_Avoid_: Related paper, project publication

**Featured paper**:
A publication the PI chose to show on the home page. Until any are chosen, the most recent publications are shown instead.
_Avoid_: Selected publication, highlighted paper
